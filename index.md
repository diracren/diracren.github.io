## Research

My research focuses on experimental nuclear and particle physics, with analysis experience in the Belle and Belle II collaborations since 2016.

### Hadron spectroscopy

Studies of excited charmed baryons and charmed-strange mesons, including the Ξc(2930), Λc(2910), and radiative decays of the D*s0(2317).

### Charmed-baryon decays

Absolute branching fractions, semileptonic decays, decay asymmetries, and tests of lepton flavor universality in charmed-baryon systems.

### Rare processes

Searches for baryon- and lepton-number violation at Belle II, and sensitivity studies of charged-lepton flavor violation at HIEPA.
